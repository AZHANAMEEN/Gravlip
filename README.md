# 🎮 [Gravlip]

Hi 👋  
I’m **Azz**, and this is a **gravity-based ball & obstacle avoidance game** I’m currently building.

The core idea is simple but unforgiving:
you don’t fight enemies — you fight **physics, timing, and your own decisions**.

---

## 🧠 Concept

You control a ball affected by gravity in a hostile environment filled with obstacles.  
There’s no hand-holding — momentum matters, mistakes compound, and survival depends on precision.

Think:
- Gravity as a mechanic, not just a rule
- Minimal visuals, maximum feedback
- Easy to understand, hard to master
- simple controls, minimal distraction

---

## ⚙️ Core Features (Current / Planned)

- 🟢 Gravity-driven ball movement  
- 🧱 Dynamic obstacle avoidance  
- 🔄 Momentum-based control (no instant stops)
- 📈 Increasing difficulty over time
- 🎯 Skill-focused gameplay (reaction > grinding)
